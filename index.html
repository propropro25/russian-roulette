What this preset represents: the initial one-in-4,000 setting comes from a historical estimate involving post-vaccinal encephalitis following primary smallpox vaccination in adults in the Netherlands. The literature review reports a wider historical range after primary smallpox vaccination. The chamber can therefore be changed rather than presenting one estimate as universal across every vaccine, population and era.
